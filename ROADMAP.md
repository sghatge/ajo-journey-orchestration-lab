# AJO learning and build roadmap

Time budget: 6–8 hours/week. AJO work follows AEP data contracts and profile assumptions; do not assume the AEP lab is a production-connected source.

| Week | Focus | Hands-on output | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AJO architecture and AEP dependency | Platform map and prerequisites | Explain profile, event, audience and journey roles |
| 2 | Events and journey entry | Event and entry specification | Define event payload, identity and re-entry rules |
| 3 | Conditions, waits and exits | Abandoned-cart journey topology | Purchase/consent/expiry exits are explicit |
| 4 | Channels and personalization | Email/SMS content data contract | Missing attributes have safe fallbacks |
| 5 | Consent and fatigue | Eligibility and suppression policy | Consent unknown/denied blocks channel action |
| 6 | Offer decisioning and experiments | Offer eligibility matrix and test plan | Margin, inventory and discount checks documented |
| 7 | QA and measurement | Test matrix and KPI definitions | Entry, delivery, conversion and opt-out metrics defined |
| 8 | Portfolio demo | Journey walkthrough and evidence pack | Reproducible simulation and clear tenant limitations |

Weekly cadence: 2 hours concepts, 2–3 hours build, 2–3 hours test/document. Live platform exercises depend on licensed sandbox access.
